Etsy → Shopify Recovery Kit

A 4-week plan and 3 ready-to-use templates to turn visitors into buyers on your new Shopify store.

How to use this kit

Do one week at a time, in order. Tick each task as you finish it (your progress is saved in this browser). Fix the basics first: trust, then product pages, then search and email. Adding more ads before the basics are fixed usually just buys more visitors who leave.

The 4-week plan

Week 1: Build trust

Why: a new store has no history

On Etsy, buyers trust the marketplace. On your own store, they must trust you.

Week 2: Fix your product pages

Why: pages decide the sale

Don't copy Etsy listings as they are. Rework your top 5 products first.

Week 3: Search and traffic

Why: Etsy sent you free visitors; Shopify doesn't

Etsy has its own search engine. On Shopify you build your own visibility.

Week 4: Email and returning customers

Why: owning your audience is the point of leaving Etsy

An email list is the one audience no marketplace can take from you.

Template 1: Announcement email

Subject: We have a new home (and a thank-you gift)

Hi [First name],

Thank you for supporting [Shop name]. It meant a lot.

I've opened my own online shop at [your-store.com], where you'll find the same handmade [product type] plus new designs that aren't available anywhere else.

As a thank-you, here is 10% off your next order with the code [CODE]. It's valid until [date].

If you have any question, just reply to this email. I read every message.

Thank you again,
[Your name]
[Shop name]
Check Etsy's rules before sending. Etsy has policies about using buyer information for marketing. Only email people who have agreed to hear from you, and use channels you own (your social accounts, packaging inserts, your own email list). Read Etsy's current policies to be sure.

Template 2: Returns & refunds policy

Returns & Refunds

We want you to love your order. If you're not happy, here's how it works.

Returns
You can return items within [30] days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

How to start a return
Email [your email] with your order number and a short note on the reason. We'll reply within [2] business days with the return instructions.

Refunds
Once we receive your return, we'll refund the item price to your original payment method within [5–7] business days. [Original shipping costs are / are not] refundable.

Damaged or wrong items
If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, email us a photo within [7] days. We'll send a replacement or refund you.

Custom or personalized items
Because they're made for you, custom items can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.

Questions?
Write to [your email]. We're happy to help.

Adapt the numbers to what you can really offer. Local consumer rules may require specific terms in your country, so check them.
